GainCurve: Level Up Fitness

Train, eat, level up — for iPhone and Apple Watch.

GainCurve turns your training and nutrition into a role-playing game. Log sets with progressive-overload targets, follow a training plan, track food and micronutrients, and earn XP and levels for the work you put in.

  • Workout logger with warm-ups, supersets, rest timer and lock-screen controls
  • Training plans, cardio timers and weekly zone 2 / zone 5 minutes
  • Food log with barcode scanner, 15,000+ foods, recipes and micronutrients
  • Adaptive calorie target from your weight trend
  • Apple Watch app with heart rate; Apple Health integration
